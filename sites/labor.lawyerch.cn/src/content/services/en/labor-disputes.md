Labour cases are often decided before arbitration begins. How the contract was signed, how pay and attendance were recorded, and how a dismissal was communicated determine how hard the evidence will be to prove.

Work starts with the documents, not the emotion. Confirm the employment relationship, the type of dispute and the limitation period first.

> General information only. A specific case needs separate assessment.
