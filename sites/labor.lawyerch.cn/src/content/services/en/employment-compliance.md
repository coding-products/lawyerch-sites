Compliance is not about eliminating risk. It is about making risk predictable, controllable and provable.

Most companies do not lack policies. They lack the democratic procedure, publication record or proof of employee acknowledgement that makes a policy usable in arbitration.

> Scope depends on company size, employment model and jurisdiction.
